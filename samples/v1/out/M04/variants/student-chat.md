第31-33题 同类练习

同类题 1

Last Sunday, Kangkang and his friends helped at the school library. They arrived at nine o'clock. Ms. Chen, the librarian, asked them to put returned books back on the shelves. Some books were very old, so the students fixed the broken covers with tape. After lunch, they made a list of new books for the reading corner. Kangkang said, "I never knew that every book has its own number." Before going home, they wrote a note on the door: "Please return books on time!"

1\. When did they arrive at the library?

A. At eight.    B. At nine.    C. After lunch.    D. In the evening.

2\. What did Kangkang learn at the library?

A. Every book has its own number.    B. How to cook lunch.    C. How to draw a map.    D. How to buy new books.

3\. What is the best title for the passage?

A. A New Librarian    B. Helping at the School Library    C. Broken Covers    D. Lunch at School

同类题 2

On Saturday, Maria and her classmates visited a community garden near their school. They got there at half past seven. An old farmer, Mr. Zhou, gave each student a small spade. The students watered the vegetables and pulled out weeds between the rows. At ten they had a rest and drank fresh juice. Later, Mr. Zhou showed them how to plant tomato seeds in small pots. Maria said, "I never knew that seeds need warm soil to grow." Before they left, they put a sign on the gate: "Please don't step on the plants!"

1\. When did they get to the garden?

A. At seven.    B. At half past seven.    C. At ten.    D. At noon.

2\. What did Mr. Zhou show the students?

A. How to make juice.    B. How to plant tomato seeds.    C. How to build a gate.    D. How to draw plants.

3\. What is the best title for the passage?

A. Fresh Juice    B. A Morning at the Community Garden    C. An Old Spade    D. Warm Soil

同类题 3

Last month, Jane's class spent a day at the science museum as young guides. They met the museum keeper at half past eight. She gave each student a badge and a map. In the morning, the students learned about the old clocks on the second floor. After lunch, they took turns to explain the clocks to younger visitors. Jane said, "I never knew that the first clocks used water." At the end of the day, the keeper thanked them with a small gift.

1\. What did the museum keeper give each student?

A. A badge and a map.    B. A small clock.    C. A ticket.    D. Some water.

2\. What did Jane learn at the museum?

A. Clocks are cheap.    B. The first clocks used water.    C. Guides need maps.    D. Museums open late.

3\. What is the best title for the passage?

A. Young Guides at the Science Museum    B. Old Maps    C. A Small Gift    D. The Second Floor
